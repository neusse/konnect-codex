# Legacy and manual-assembly sourcing

For legacy, surplus, socketed, or hand-assembled parts, add these checks to the
manufacturing package:

- Exact manufacturer part number and suffix, lifecycle confidence, source, and
  date checked.
- At least one functionally and mechanically evaluated alternate, or an
  explicit no-alternate risk.
- Package, lead finish, lead diameter, pitch/tolerance, polarity/key, height,
  and keepout.
- Socket part number and dimensions when replaceability is required; otherwise
  state direct-solder intent and rework risk.
- Hand-solder access, thermal mass, pad/drill allowance, insertion orientation,
  and silkscreen markings visible after assembly.
- Counterfeit, aged-stock, oxidation, and mixed-suffix risks for reseller or
  surplus inventory.
- Quantity on hand, attrition allowance, and replacement plan.

Do not treat a distributor listing as proof of active manufacturer production.
Capture the evidence and uncertainty in the BOM/assembly notes.
